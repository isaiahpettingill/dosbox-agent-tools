# Fictional Filler Floppies

These are fictional novelty software products for future small DOS floppy
projects. They are not real products, endorsements, or vendor packages.

- `MacroHard MagicBASIC!`: an allegedly friendly beginner language with a
  questionable spell-checker and magical error messages.
- `KhanKhan's Novelty Floppies`: a rotating catalog of joke utilities, sound
  effects, fake screensavers, and impractical holiday software.
- `TurboTortoise Defrag`: a very slow disk organizer that offers contemplative
  advice while arranging files.
- `GalactiCalc Deluxe`: a cargo, pizza, and interstellar-distance calculator.
- `PhantomHustlers Address Book`: an address manager with a questionable
  paranormal contact category and the slogan "Who you gonna email?"
- `SporeDOS Home Lab`: a fictional mushroom observation notebook. It must not
  provide real chemistry or biology instructions.

Keep future implementations small, 8086-safe, and self-contained. Package each
as its own floppy only when it has a concrete interactive behavior worth
demonstrating.
